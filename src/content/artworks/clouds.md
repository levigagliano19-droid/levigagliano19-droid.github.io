CLOUDS captures that restless movement in physical form. Waves of blue emerge from the canvas surface, twisting and drifting across it like clouds caught in changing winds. The raised, irregular texture gives the piece a sense of motion - as if the sky itself has been pulled out of the distance and brought into the room.

The work explores the atmosphere's energy: unpredictable, expansive, and impossible to hold in one fixed shape.

Nothing in the sky stays still. CLOUDS is a moment of that movement, frozen in form.
