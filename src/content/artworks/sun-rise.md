SUN RISE

At the center of the piece, energy erupts outward.

Inspired by the sun as a source of constant movement and force, this work transforms its presence into a physical, sculptural form. The raised surface expands and twists away from the center, creating the feeling of heat, motion, and energy radiating in every direction.

The rough, imperfect texture is intentional. Rather than presenting the sun as something distant or untouchable, the piece brings its force into the physical world - something you can see, feel, and almost imagine moving.

This piece represents energy at its most powerful: continuous, immense, and impossible to contain.
