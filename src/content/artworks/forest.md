Roots push through the earth. Branches reach toward the light. Wind moves through the canopy. Everything grows, shifts, competes, and adapts.

FOREST takes that constant movement and brings it into physical form. The deep green tones and rough, protruding surface create the feeling of something growing beyond the limits of the canvas - organic, unpredictable, and alive.

The piece explores the quiet strength of nature: an energy that doesn't need to be loud to be powerful.

FOREST is a study of growth, movement, and the forces that continue beneath the surface.
